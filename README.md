# Swoop

**Beautiful screen recordings with auto-zoom. Free, open source, made for Windows.**

</div>

> [!WARNING]
> Swoop is in early development and there is nothing to download yet.
> Star or watch the repo to hear when the first release lands.

Swoop turns plain screen recordings into polished demo videos, automatically.
It is inspired by [Screen Studio](https://screen.studio) and built for Windows.

## What it will do

- **Auto-zoom.** The camera zooms in when you click and follows your cursor smoothly.
- **Smooth cursor.** Your real cursor is left out of the capture and replaced by a smooth, scalable one with click effects.
- **Pretty by default.** Wallpapers and gradients, padding, rounded corners and shadows.
- **Timeline editor.** Trim, cut, speed up, and fine-tune every zoom.
- **Export.** MP4 up to 4K at 60 fps, plus GIF, in landscape, vertical or square.
- **Private.** No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Your recordings never leave your PC.
